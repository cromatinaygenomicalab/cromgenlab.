Dr. Miguel Santibáñez Andrade

Associate investigator at the Chromatin Regulation and Genomics Laboratory (CromGenLab), Instituto Nacional de Cancerología (INCan), Mexico. He leads projects on the effect of environmental pollution on histone chaperones and on quality of life in testicular cancer survivors.

Student projects he supervises

  • Effect of PM2.5 and PM10 on the ATRX/DAXX complex in A549 cells
  • Effect of particulate matter on DAXX expression and chromosomal instability
  • Quality of life, unmet needs and financial toxicity in testicular cancer survivors
