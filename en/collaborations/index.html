Collaborations

CromGenLab works with clinical groups at INCan, Mexican universities and research centers in Europe and the United States. It also works on the neurobiology of aging, axolotl regeneration and environmental pollution.

International consortia

TECACTesticular Cancer ConsortiumDr. Rodrigo González Barrios de la Parra is a member of this international consortium that pools genome-wide association studies (GWAS) to identify genetic risk markers for testicular germ cell tumors.med.upenn.edu/tecac →
