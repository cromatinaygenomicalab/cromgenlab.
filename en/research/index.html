Five lines

From molecule to patient

I

Epigenetic chromatin remodeling in cancer

histone modifications · chaperones · environment

Epigenetic mechanisms that regulate the organization and establishment of chromatin, and their alterations in the development and progression of cancer and other diseases. We analyze epigenetic modifications, histone variants and histone-depositing chaperones, and their impact on gene regulation in response to environmental factors.

II

Histone variants and chaperones in cell transformation

DAXX · ATRX · HIRA · H3.3

How changes in histone variants and their chaperones affect genomic stability and drive the abnormal gene expression of different cancer types. We look for mechanisms that could become therapeutic or diagnostic targets.

III

Molecular and epigenetic signatures in testicular germ cell tumors

TGCT · cisplatin · chemoresistance

Genetic variants and epigenetic alterations associated with response or resistance to chemotherapy in patients with testicular germ cell tumors.

IV

Prognostic and predictive biomarkers through integrative multimodal analysis

radiomics · genomics · epigenomics

Deep learning, radiomics and genomic and epigenomic analysis to identify genetic and imaging biomarkers that improve diagnosis, prognosis and treatment prediction in cancer and other diseases.

V

Artificial intelligence applied to biomedicine

omics data · biomedical imaging · personalized medicine

Deep learning algorithms to analyze large volumes of omics data and biomedical images, and to integrate molecular, epigenetic and radiological information.
