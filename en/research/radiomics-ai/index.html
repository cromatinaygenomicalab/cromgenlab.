Lines IV and V · Imaging and artificial intelligence

Radiomics and artificial intelligence

CromGenLab applies radiomics, computer vision and deep learning to identify imaging biomarkers and integrate them with clinical, genomic and epigenomic data in pursuit of personalized medicine.

← Research
Research linesChromatin & epigeneticsTESTINCanRadiomics & AI
IV

Prognostic and predictive biomarkers through integrative multimodal analysis

radiomics · genomics · epigenomics

Deep learning, radiomics and genomic and epigenomic analysis to identify genetic and imaging biomarkers that improve diagnosis, prognosis and treatment prediction in cancer and other diseases.

V

Artificial intelligence applied to biomedicine

omics data · biomedical imaging · personalized medicine

Deep learning algorithms to analyze large volumes of omics data and biomedical images, and to integrate molecular, epigenetic and radiological information.

Computer vision

From CT scan to biomarker

CT radiomics

Quantitative shape, intensity and texture features of the tumor and host tissues, extracted from computed tomography scans.

Imaging biomarkers

Deep learning models, including convolutional neural networks, that estimate biological traits of the patient from images.

Applications

  • Testicular germ cell tumors: prediction of risk, prognosis and platinum resistance in the TESTINCan.
  • Sarcomas: radiomic markers of relapse in advanced synovial sarcoma.
  • Ovarian cancer: imaging-based predictive models of treatment resistance.
