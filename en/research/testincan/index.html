TESTINCan: testicular germ cell tumors

TESTINCan is CromGenLab’s clinical, molecular and imaging platform to study risk, prognosis and response to cisplatin chemotherapy in testicular germ cell tumors (TGCT), the most common solid cancer in young men.

← Research
Research linesChromatin & epigeneticsTESTINCanRadiomics & AI

More than 4,000 patients

The cohort brings together patients with TGCT treated at the Instituto Nacional de Cancerología between 2007 and 2025. Each patient is described by layers of clinical, biological, molecular, environmental and imaging data that are integrated to search for biomarkers of risk, prognosis and platinum resistance.

>4,000patients treated at INCan between 2007 and 2025

Risk

Likelihood of developing the disease compared with the general population.

Prognosis

Likely course of the disease, independent of treatment.

Response

Telling cisplatin-sensitive patients apart from resistant ones.

Data layers

Each source gives rise to several data layers that are integrated to search for biomarkers.

Sociodemographic dataSocioeconomic status and place of residence
Body composition and metabolismBMI, skeletal muscle, metabolic profile

Biobank
Biological samplesTumor, adjacent tissue, serum, plasma and packed red cells
GenomicsWhole exome (WES) of tumor and peripheral blood · aCGH
EpigenomicsDNA methylation (Illumina EPIC v2) · chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq)
TranscriptomicsTumor RNA-seq
Imaging & AI
Computer visionAutomated analysis of patient images with deep learning
CT radiomicsShape, intensity and texture of the tumor and host tissues
Imaging biomarkersDeep learning models that estimate biological traits of the patient from images
